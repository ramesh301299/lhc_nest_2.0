5. Purpose of Processing

The Company processes personal data collected through the Application solely for the following lawful purposes related to the administration of its rental properties:

  • Tenancy Formation and Verification: To evaluate enquiries and bookings, verify the identity and address of prospective tenants, assess eligibility, and execute rental agreements.
  • Contractual Performance: To perform the rental agreement between the tenant and the lessor, including allotment of the unit, application of agreed rent and escalation, administration of the lock-in and notice periods, renewals, and handover on vacating.
  • Rent Collection and Accounting: To raise and issue rent receipts, record payments and the bank account in which they were received, maintain the tenant ledger, compute dues and arrears, and reconcile collections.
  • Deposits, Advances and Refunds: To record security deposits and advances, track instalment schedules, assess deductions on vacating, and process refunds.
  • Property Condition and Dispute Prevention: To maintain a photographic record of the condition of a unit at move-in and move-out, so that responsibility for damage and the resulting deductions can be assessed fairly and evidenced if disputed.
  • Maintenance and Expense Management: To record maintenance requests, expenses, vendor bills and fixture purchases attributable to a property, unit or tenancy.
  • Statutory and Tax Compliance: To compute and report Goods and Services Tax where applicable, support Tax Deducted at Source obligations, maintain books of account, and respond to statutory audits and assessments.
  • Communication with Tenants: To prepare rent reminders, receipt intimations, renewal notices and similar operational messages for despatch by authorised staff.
  • Reporting and Business Analytics: To produce occupancy, collection, arrears and profitability reports for internal management decision-making.
  • Security, Integrity and Fraud Prevention: To authenticate users, enforce role-based access, maintain an audit trail of changes to financial and tenancy records, and detect and prevent unauthorised access or fraudulent entries.
  • Legal Claims and Dispute Resolution: To establish, exercise or defend legal claims arising from a tenancy, including recovery of arrears, eviction proceedings and deposit disputes.
  • Technical Support and Improvement: To provide technical assistance, diagnose faults, and improve the functionality, performance and reliability of the Application.

The Company shall not use personal data for any purpose beyond those explicitly stated in this Policy without obtaining prior consent from the Data Principal, except where permitted or required by law. Personal data collected through the Application is never used for advertising, profiling for commercial gain, or sale to any third party.

8. Aadhaar, PAN and Identity Document Disclosure

The Application records statutory identifiers and identity documents furnished by tenants for the purpose of verifying identity before a tenancy is granted. Data Principals are hereby informed of the following:

8.1 Purpose of Collection

Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN and uploaded identity or address proof documents are collected and retained solely to:

  • Verify the identity and address of a tenant, joint tenant, or the authorised representative of a corporate tenant, before allotment of a unit;
  • Correctly identify the counterparty named in the rental agreement and in receipts issued against it;
  • Satisfy statutory identification, tax and reporting obligations, including PAN quotation and GST particulars where applicable;
  • Support any tenant verification or intimation requirement imposed by the police or local authority in the jurisdiction of the property.

8.2 Voluntary Nature and Alternatives

Furnishing an Aadhaar number is voluntary. Where a tenant declines to furnish Aadhaar, the Company will accept an alternative officially valid document — such as a passport, driving licence, voter identity card, or such other document as the Company may reasonably accept — for the purpose of identity and address verification. 8.3 Handling and Restrictions

  • Identity document numbers and images are accessible only to authenticated staff of the Company acting on a need-to-know basis, and are never exposed to tenants, to other tenants, or to any external party through the Application;
  • The Application does not perform Aadhaar authentication, e-KYC, Yes/No verification, or any transaction with the Central Identities Data Repository, and is not an Aadhaar requesting entity;
  • Aadhaar numbers are never used as a login identifier, a customer number, or a key for matching records across systems;
  • Identity documents are not shared with any third party for commercial purposes, and are disclosed only as described in Section 10;
  • Where an identity document is no longer required and no legal obligation compels its retention, the Company removes it in accordance with Section 12.

8.4 Correction and Objection

A Data Principal may at any time request correction of an inaccurate identifier, request that a superseded identity document be removed, or object to the continued retention of a particular document, by contacting the Grievance Officer as set out in Section 16. 12. Data Retention

Lovely Home Creators retains personal data for the duration necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal and regulatory obligations, and maintain accurate business records. Specific retention periods are as follows:

12.1 Active Tenancies

Personal data relating to a current tenant is retained for the duration of the tenancy and of any renewal, and is kept current so that agreements, receipts and ledgers remain accurate.

12.2 Concluded Tenancies

Following the expiry or termination of a tenancy and the settlement of the deposit, tenancy and financial records may be retained for a period of up to eight (8) years, or such longer period as may be required under applicable law, in order to:

  • Comply with the record-keeping requirements of the Goods and Services Tax laws and the Income-tax Act, 1961;
  • Support statutory audit, tax assessment and reassessment proceedings;
  • Establish, exercise or defend legal claims arising from the tenancy, including claims within the limitation period prescribed by the Limitation Act, 1963;
  • Maintain a complete and auditable accounting history of the property.

12.3 Identity Documents

Uploaded identity and address proof documents are retained for the duration of the tenancy and for such period thereafter as is required to defend a claim relating to the identity of the counterparty, and are then deleted. Where a Data Principal requests earlier removal of a superseded document and no legal obligation requires its retention, the Company will act on that request.

12.4 Condition Photographs

Move-in and move-out photographs are retained until the security deposit has been settled and any dispute concerning damage has been concluded, and for a reasonable period thereafter to allow for a claim to be raised. They are then deleted, save where they form part of the record in a pending proceeding.

12.5 Prospective Tenants Who Do Not Proceed

Where an enquiry or booking does not result in a tenancy, the personal data of the prospective tenant is retained only for so long as is necessary to conclude the enquiry, and in any event for no more than twelve (12) months, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

12.6 Audit Trail, Technical Logs and Diagnostic Data

The audit trail of changes to tenancy and financial records is retained for the same period as the underlying record, because it forms part of that record's integrity. Technical logs, error reports and diagnostic data are retained for a period of up to one (1) year, or such shorter period as is necessary for troubleshooting, security monitoring, and Application improvement.

12.7 Data Deletion

Upon expiration of the applicable retention period, personal data is securely deleted or anonymised in accordance with industry best practices. Deletion includes removal from active databases, backup systems, and archival storage, subject to technical feasibility and legal constraints. Data Principals may request deletion of their personal data in accordance with Section 18 of this Policy, subject to the Company's legal and contractual obligations.

13. Security Safeguards

Lovely Home Creators and SDK Infinity Technologies implement reasonable technical, organizational, and physical security measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, destruction, loss, or misuse. Security safeguards include, but are not limited to:

13.1 Technical Security Measures

  • Encryption of personal data in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS) protocols
  • Encryption of personal data at rest using advanced encryption algorithms
  • Secure authentication mechanisms, with credentials stored only in hashed form
  • Signed, expiring access tokens for the mobile application, which are validated against a server-side record and can be revoked individually
  • Enforcement of every access request against the user's own permissions and record-level rules, so that the mobile application can never retrieve a record the same user could not see in the web back office
  • Regular security vulnerability assessments and penetration testing
  • Firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and network security monitoring
  • Secure application development practices, including code reviews and security testing

13.2 Organizational Security Measures

  • Role-based access controls restricting access to personal data on a need-to-know basis, with deletion of records and access to billing entity and statutory reporting data reserved to the Administrator role
  • Record-level scoping by company and billing entity, so that staff see only the portfolio they administer
  • An immutable audit trail on tenancy and financial records, recording who changed what and when
  • Confidentiality obligations imposed on staff, contractors, and service providers
  • Regular training and awareness programs on data protection and security best practices
  • Incident response procedures for detecting, reporting, and mitigating security breaches
  • Data minimisation practices to limit collection and retention of personal data
  • Vendor management and due diligence processes for third-party service providers

13.3 Physical Security Measures

  • Secure data centres with restricted physical access controls, surveillance systems, and environmental protections
  • Secure disposal of physical media and printed records containing personal data
  • Backup and disaster recovery procedures to ensure business continuity and data availability

13.4 Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach, the Company shall notify the affected Data Principals and the Data Protection Board of India in the manner and within the timelines prescribed under the DPDP Act, 2023 and the rules made thereunder, and shall take prompt remedial action to contain the breach and mitigate harm.

13.5 Limitation of Liability

While the Company employs reasonable security measures, no system is entirely immune to security risks. The Company cannot guarantee absolute security of personal data transmitted over the internet or stored in digital systems. Data Principals acknowledge and accept the inherent risks associated with electronic data transmission and storage.

15. Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

Data Principals are entitled to exercise the following rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, subject to applicable legal limitations and exceptions:

Data Principals have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning them is being processed and, where applicable, to obtain a summary of such personal data, of the processing activities undertaken, and of the identities of the parties with whom it has been shared. 15.2 Right to Correction and Completion

Data Principals have the right to request correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data, completion of incomplete personal data, and updating of outdated personal data — for example, a change of contact number, a corrected spelling of a name, or an updated permanent address. The Company shall verify the corrected information and update its records accordingly.

15.3 Right to Erasure

Data Principals have the right to request erasure of personal data where retention is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal, regulatory, tax, accounting or contractual obligations that require continued retention. Erasure requests are evaluated on a case-by-case basis, and the Company will explain the reason where a request cannot be fully met.

15.4 Right to Data Portability

Data Principals have the right to receive personal data concerning them in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format and to transmit such data to another data fiduciary, where technically feasible and permitted by law.

15.5 Right to Grievance Redressal

Data Principals have the right to file grievances regarding the processing of personal data with the designated Grievance Officer of Lovely Home Creators, and, where the grievance is not satisfactorily resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India, as provided under the DPDP Act.

15.6 Right to Nominate
