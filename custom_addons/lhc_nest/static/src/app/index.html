Bill Before Payment

Send tenants what they owe before they pay — works for GST and non-GST tenants alike
📌 Proforma Invoice is just an estimate, not a tax document — nothing about it requires the tenant to be GST-registered. Any tenant can get one, including individuals. Tax Invoice (before payment) is the one that's GST-specific, since it uses your real GST numbering.
📅Monthly Billing Run
1st of every month
📌 Run this on the 1st to generate every active tenant's bill in one go — but sharing is still one tenant at a time. There's no WhatsApp/SMS integration; office staff copy or download each tenant's PDF and paste it into that tenant's own WhatsApp chat manually. "Generate All" prepares the documents and numbers; "Copy" per row is what actually gets shared.
